ABOUT ME

06+YEARS
EXPERIENCE

Design with purpose,
storytelling with soul.

I’m Prabhjot Kaur, a Visual Designer & Illustrator with 6 years of experience creating engaging, meaningful, and visually impactful designs.

My work spans visual identity, branding & logo design, social media, illustration, character design, educational & print design, infographics, merchandise, and UI/UX design.

I enjoy transforming ideas into creative visual experiences that communicate clearly, tell stories, and connect with audiences. My design approach combines creativity, visual storytelling, attention to detail, and clear communication.

Over the years, I’ve worked on a variety of projects for brands, campaigns, educational content, digital platforms, and communication materials, adapting my visual style to different audiences and requirements.

I’m always excited to explore new ideas, experiment with different visual styles, and take on creative challenges.
